Checkout flakiness — what we found

task t-shot-01agent.mason2026-08-28T13:13:19.868Z

Goal

Stop the checkout suite failing at random in CI.

What was built

A deterministic fixture and a seeded clock, behind one helper.

Decisions

Seed the clock rather than retry the test.

Trade-offs

A seeded clock can hide a real timing bug; a retry hides every bug. We took the narrower risk.

Evidence

200 consecutive runs, 0 failures. Previously 7 failures in 200.

Open questions

Should the seed be per-suite or per-case? Per-case is stricter but noisier.
