# Memory — {{agentId}}

This file is your long-term memory. It survives your process, your session and
your respawn: what is written here is handed back to you the next time you are
started, and it is the only thing that is.

Write to it yourself, in your own words. Append a new dated section — never edit
or delete what is already here, including your own earlier entries. You are
writing for the version of you that comes back tomorrow with no other memory of
today.

Worth an entry: what you learned about this codebase, a decision and why it went
that way, something that surprised you, a mistake and how you found it. Not
worth an entry: what you did step by step — the event log already has that.
